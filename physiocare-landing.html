Frequently asked questions

Do I need an appointment?

Appointments help us give you dedicated time. Send a request and our team will confirm a slot.

What happens during my first visit?

Your physiotherapist listens to your history and goals, assesses movement, and discusses a suggested plan with you.

How long is a typical session?

Session length varies by service. The team will confirm the duration when your slot is confirmed.

What should I bring?

Bring any reports or prescriptions you have, and wear comfortable clothing you can move in.

Can I request a specific physiotherapist?

Yes. Mention your preference in your request and we will do our best to accommodate it.

Do you offer home physiotherapy?

Home physiotherapy is listed as a service. Send an enquiry to check availability in your area.

Can I reschedule?

Yes. Contact the clinic by phone, WhatsApp or email and we will help you find a new time.

How do I contact the clinic?

Use the form below, call, message on WhatsApp or send an email.
